## CSSP Brazil Annual Workshop 2026 Agenda

#### 16 – 18 June 2026

INPE CPTEC Cachoeira Paulista, São Paulo **Overview** The CSSP Brazil Annual Science Workshop is a chance for UK and Brazilian scientists involved in CSSP Brazil collaboration to come together to review progress, raise awareness of work going on across the partnership and develop linkages across partners and work packages.

We will discuss science progress, highlights and plans, strengthen partnerships between individuals, work packages and institutions, and identify opportunities for new demonstration climate services.

It is also an opportunity to identify project impacts, including benefits to Brazil, the UK and the international science community and showcase our results to stakeholders and potential decision makers.

We will look ahead to scope future opportunities to continue working together to tackle outstanding scientific challenges and further strengthen the CSSP Brazil project to build a legacy of science to service capability.

Information about the workshop, venue and registration process can be found on the CSSP Brazil UK Workshop SharePoint page. **Outline agenda**
