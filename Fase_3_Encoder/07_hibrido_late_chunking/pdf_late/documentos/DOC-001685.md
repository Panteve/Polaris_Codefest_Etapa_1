**The Armed Forces**

**Specifi c Missions**

**Army**

The Argentine Army shall serve the Motherland to contribute to national defence and protect its vital interests: including the nation’s independence and sovereignty, self-determination, territorial integrity; its natural resources, protection of assets, the life and freedom of its inhabitants. Likewise, it shall also contribute to maintaining the republican representative and federal system of government.
