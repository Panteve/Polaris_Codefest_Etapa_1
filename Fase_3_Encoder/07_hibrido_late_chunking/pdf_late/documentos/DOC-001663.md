**Specialized Port Security Body: (CESEP):** It is responsible for security and the protection of the national port system. Its mission is to provide the Port Authority with the highest level of security for the county’s port installations, whether they are nationalised, operating under concessions, or private. Furthermore, it is the implementing agency for the protection of Boats and Port Installations.

**Specialized Ground Border Security Body (CESFRONT):** Its mission is to establish permanent security and control in the country’s formal entry and exit points as well as areas assigned to it along the Dominican land border. It performs patrols and establishes checkpoints, observation points and screening in coordination with the various agencies deployed along the Dominican-Haitian border.
